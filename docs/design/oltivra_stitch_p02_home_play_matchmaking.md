# Oltivra — Stitch Package 02: Tutorial, Home and Match Entry

## Use

Use only after Package 01 is approved. This package defines the first post-onboarding experience and match entry, not live gameplay.

---

## START OF STITCH PROMPT

Apply the approved **Oltivra Design Foundation** and preserve the visual language already approved in Package 01.

Generate **exactly six high-fidelity portrait mobile screens** for **Package 02 — Tutorial, Home and Match Entry**. Use English UI copy and the approved light premium visual system.

Do not create any screens beyond the six below. Do not add category selection, custom public rooms, chat, voice, player-created questions, bots labels, stores, currencies, a map, or extra game modes. Outside live matches, use the approved five-item bottom navigation: **Home · Play · Rankings · Social · Profile**.

### Screen 1 — One-screen game guide

- Title: **“Two ways to rise”**.
- Present exactly two clearly separated mode summaries:
  - **Quick Battle** — “4 players · 10 questions · first correct answer scores.”
  - **Survival** — “10 players · wrong or no answer eliminates.”
- Use small, elegant abstract illustrations or mini UI diagrams, not cartoon characters or classroom imagery.
- Primary button: **Let’s play**.
- Do not add a multi-slide carousel, tutorial video, practice mode, or skip/next sequence.

### Screen 2 — Home

- Bottom navigation is visible with **Home** active.
- Compact top area: selected avatar, username, level, and a small league chip. Do not show email, followers, real name, location, or raw MMR.
- Dominant primary card/button: **Join a Quick Battle** in turquoise.
- Secondary, clearly smaller path: **Try Survival**.
- Beneath it, include only two compact areas:
  - **Today’s missions** with two brief progress rows.
  - **This week** with the player’s rank preview and a route to Rankings.
- Keep Home spacious. Do not add news feed, announcement banner, carousel, daily spin, shop, ad, list of every feature, or large analytics dashboard.

### Screen 3 — Play mode selection

- Bottom navigation is visible with **Play** active.
- Title: **“Choose your battle”**.
- Present exactly two large mode cards:
  - **Quick Battle**: “4 players · 10 questions · 11 seconds each.”
  - **Survival**: “10 players · last player standing.”
- Both cards use the same component family. Quick Battle has a turquoise emphasis; Survival uses restrained yellow/pink energy without looking like a warning state.
- Show a compact neutral line: **“Mixed questions · English”**. Do not turn it into a selector.
- Each card has one clear CTA: **Play Quick Battle** or **Play Survival**.
- Do not add category filters, difficulty filters, private code entry, custom room controls, paid boosts, or solo play.

### Screen 4 — Quick Battle matchmaking

- No bottom navigation.
- Heading: **“Finding your Quick Battle”**.
- Four compact roster slots, initially in a calm searching state.
- Supporting rules: **“4 players · 10 questions · 11 seconds each.”**
- One secondary text action: **Cancel**.
- Use a restrained sense of live activity, not a large spinning wheel or arcade animation.
- Do not show whether any slot is a human or bot, do not label AI, and do not expose player rating, location, or queue time.

### Screen 5 — Quick Battle match ready

- No bottom navigation.
- Four filled roster slots with curated avatars and short usernames; do not label bots or humans.
- Prominent but compact countdown: **“Starts in 3”**.
- Small context line: **“Quick Battle · English questions”**.
- Keep the screen focused and quiet; there is no chat, profile tap flow, reaction picker, ad, or mode change here.

### Screen 6 — Survival match ready

- No bottom navigation.
- Ten compact roster slots arranged clearly without clutter; use curated avatars and short usernames only.
- Prominent compact countdown: **“Starts in 3”**.
- Supporting line: **“Survival · last player standing”**.
- Do not display a bracket, prize, entry fee, revive, character power, or a label that distinguishes bots from people.

### Package guardrails

- Preserve the same user avatar, wordmark treatment, colour semantics, spacing, and bottom navigation across applicable screens.
- Make the player’s next action immediately obvious on Screens 1–4. Screens 5–6 have no action other than waiting for the live match to begin.
- End after these six screens. Do not design active questions, answer states, results, social screens, or settings yet.

## END OF STITCH PROMPT
