# Oltivra — Stitch Package 06: Rankings, Progress and Profile

## Use

Use only after Package 05 is approved. This package designs retention and identity without creating a loot, collection, or store system.

---

## START OF STITCH PROMPT

Apply the approved **Oltivra Design Foundation** and preserve approved navigation, avatar, and status components.

Generate **exactly six high-fidelity portrait mobile screens** for **Package 06 — Rankings, Progress and Profile**. Use English UI copy. Outside matches, show the approved bottom navigation and make the selected destination obvious.

Oltivra rewards competition and consistency through XP, levels, leagues, weekly rankings, win streaks, Survival crowns, missions, badges, frames, and category statistics. None of these grant gameplay power. Do not add loot boxes, currencies, paid boosts, league chests, daily spins, season passes, shops, pets, maps, or collectible mechanics.

### Screen 1 — Weekly rankings

- Bottom navigation with **Rankings** active.
- Header: **“Rankings”** with a calm time context such as **“This week · Global”**.
- Show a ranked list with place, curated avatar, username, compact league treatment, and weekly competitive progress. Pin the current player clearly without making it visually loud.
- Do not display country rankings, precise location, raw MMR, prize pool, cash reward, wagering, or a global activity feed.

### Screen 2 — League status

- Bottom navigation with **Rankings** active.
- Make the player’s current league the visual focus, for example **Silver League**.
- Show concise progress toward the next league using an abstract progression treatment; do not expose raw MMR or invent a separate league currency.
- Include brief context: **“Ranked battles move your league progress.”**
- Do not add league chests, season battle pass, paid promotion, relegation scare tactics, or claimed reward carousel.

### Screen 3 — Missions

- Bottom navigation with **Home** active, or keep this as a clear reachable sub-screen from Home.
- Header: **“Missions”**.
- Separate exactly two sections: **Daily** and **Weekly**.
- Show concise, server-defined progress rows such as **“Play 3 Quick Battles”** and **“Answer 10 questions correctly”**.
- A completed mission may show one restrained **Claim** action. Do not show mystery boxes, double mission progress via ads, currency, paid skips, or a mission board map.

### Screen 4 — My profile

- Bottom navigation with **Profile** active.
- Present the player’s curated avatar, username, selected frame, level, league, current/best Quick Battle streak, and Survival crowns.
- Add compact routes to **Category stats**, **Achievements**, and **Edit profile**.
- Keep it like a confident player card, not a dense dashboard. Do not show real name, email, location, birth date, followers, user bio, social feed, or gameplay-power inventory.

### Screen 5 — Category statistics

- Bottom navigation with **Profile** active.
- Header: **“Category stats”**.
- Show the nine approved categories in a clean vertical list with an accessible simple accuracy/progress treatment, such as a bar plus percentage and matches played.
- Keep the layout readable; avoid a radar chart, dense data table, colour-only meaning, or a new category taxonomy.
- Do not add Gaming as a category.

### Screen 6 — Achievements and cosmetics

- Bottom navigation with **Profile** active.
- Header: **“Achievements”**.
- Show a curated, compact grid/list of earned badges and cosmetic frames, with one selected frame preview and an **Equip** action.
- Make it clear these are cosmetic/recognition only. No rarity economy, inventory capacity, exchange, currency, purchase, random opening, or loot box.
- Do not add an avatar marketplace; avatar selection and frame use remain a simple profile customisation.

### Package guardrails

- Keep weekly ranking, league, missions, and profile visually related but avoid reusing one giant dashboard on every screen.
- Use status colours sparingly: turquoise for actions/current selection, yellow for earned/reward moments, pink for competitive energy, green for completed/success states.
- End after these six screens. Do not design safety, account settings, ad/privacy consent, purchases, or reconnection states yet.

## END OF STITCH PROMPT
