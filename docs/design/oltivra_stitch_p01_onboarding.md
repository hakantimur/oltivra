# Oltivra — Stitch Package 01: First-Run Account Setup

## Use

Give this to Stitch only after it has acknowledged `oltivra_stitch_00_design_foundation.md`. This package contains exactly six first-run screens.

---

## START OF STITCH PROMPT

Apply the approved **Oltivra Design Foundation** without changing it.

Generate **exactly six high-fidelity portrait mobile screens** for **Package 01 — First-Run Account Setup**. Use the Oltivra light visual system, English UI copy, warm-white canvas, turquoise primary action, compact premium typography, and generous spacing.

Do not generate a desktop view, an app map, a prototype, extra states, a mascot, a logo mark, an additional sign-in method, or any screen beyond the six named below. This is an account-setup flow, so there is **no bottom navigation**.

### Screen 1 — Launch

- A quiet, polished launch screen on the warm-white background.
- Show the text wordmark **Oltivra** and the line **“Think fast. Rise higher.”**
- Use only a restrained abstract turquoise/pink/yellow accent; no character, trophy, game board, star field, or animated-looking effect.
- No button and no product feature list.

### Screen 2 — 13+ declaration

- Heading: **“Before you play”**.
- Plain language: **“Oltivra is for players aged 13 and over.”**
- One required confirmation control: **“I confirm I am 13 or older.”**
- Primary button: **Continue**.
- Do not ask for a date of birth, parental permission, country, school, phone number, or personal profile details.

### Screen 3 — Terms and privacy acceptance

- Heading: **“A quick agreement”**.
- Show two concise, clearly tappable rows: **Terms of Service** and **Privacy Policy**.
- Show the required acceptance sentence: **“I agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.”**
- Primary button: **Agree and continue**.
- Make it trustworthy and minimal; do not add a marketing consent screen, newsletter option, advertising offer, or long legal text wall.

### Screen 4 — Sign in or create account

- Heading: **“Ready to play?”**
- Supporting line: **“Sign in to join live battles and keep your progress.”**
- Provide exactly these three entry methods, in this order:
  1. **Continue with Google**
  2. **Continue with Apple**
  3. **Continue with email**
- Keep the buttons equally clear and calm. Apple must not be hidden or visually downgraded.
- Do not add guest play, phone sign-in, Facebook, X, Discord, passkeys, invite code, or referral entry.

### Screen 5 — Unique username

- Heading: **“Choose your player name”**.
- Supporting line: **“This is how other players will see you.”**
- One focused username input with a realistic available example such as `mira_moves`.
- Show only this concise rule line: **“3–16 characters · letters, numbers, and _ only”**.
- Include a small successful availability state: **“Name available”**.
- Primary button: **Continue**.
- Do not show @ handles, real names, date of birth, social links, bio, location, public follower counts, or a public profile preview.

### Screen 6 — Curated avatar selection

- Heading: **“Pick your avatar”**.
- Show a curated grid of 8–12 original, abstract-but-personal avatar illustrations. They should feel mature enough for 13+, diverse in colour and shape, and consistent with Oltivra’s palette.
- One avatar is visibly selected with a clear turquoise selection treatment.
- Primary button: **Start playing**.
- Include only a short helper line: **“You can change this later.”**
- Do not show camera upload, photo upload, AI avatar generation, marketplace, currency, loot boxes, pets, character names, or frames in this screen.

### Package guardrails

- Use the Oltivra text wordmark only; do not invent a separate logo symbol.
- Keep all UI copy concise and globally understandable.
- Keep the same 20 px mobile side margin, rounded controls, restrained icons, and low-shadow surfaces from the foundation.
- End after these six screens. Do not design the tutorial, Home, game modes, live match, social area, or Profile yet.
