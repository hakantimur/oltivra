# Oltivra — Stitch Package 08: Ads, Purchase and Reliable Recovery

## Use

Use only after Package 07 is approved. This is the final core package. It covers monetisation and technical recovery without disrupting fair gameplay.

---

## START OF STITCH PROMPT

Apply the approved **Oltivra Design Foundation** and preserve all earlier approved screens and components.

Generate **exactly six high-fidelity portrait mobile screens** for **Package 08 — Ads, Purchase and Reliable Recovery**. Use English UI copy. These are supportive states, not new gameplay or commerce systems.

Oltivra has no pay-to-win mechanism. Rewarded video may grant one additional copy of base match XP only; it never changes score, MMR, league, weekly rank, mission progress, placement, streak, crown, answers, or survival. There is one non-consumable **Remove Ads Forever** purchase. External interstitial ads are supplied by the ad platform and must never be designed as Oltivra content or block navigation.

### Screen 1 — Privacy and ad preferences

- Header: **“Privacy & ad preferences”**.
- Explain in plain language that the player can manage advertising privacy choices where applicable.
- Include one clear route: **Manage ad preferences**.
- Keep this an app-owned settings overview; do not imitate or recreate a third-party legal consent dialog.
- Do not add tracking toggles that promise functionality outside the approved ad/privacy system, marketing subscriptions, data-selling language, or confusing dense legal text.

### Screen 2 — Optional rewarded XP offer

- A post-match Oltivra overlay/sheet, not a full-screen interruption.
- Heading: **“Double your match XP?”**
- Supporting text: **“Watch an optional video to earn +[base match XP] XP.”**
- Mandatory clarification: **“XP only — your rank, league, streak, and mission progress will not change.”**
- Primary action: **Watch video**.
- Secondary action: **Not now**.
- Do not use countdown pressure, fake scarcity, spin wheel, currency, revive, answer help, or any competitive advantage.

### Screen 3 — Remove Ads Forever

- Header: **“Remove Ads Forever”**.
- Communicate a simple one-time benefit: **“Enjoy Oltivra without interstitial ads.”**
- Clearly state that it does not affect game outcomes or optional rewarded-video availability.
- Primary purchase button uses a localized store-price placeholder such as **Remove ads · [localized price]**.
- Include a quiet **Restore purchases** action.
- Do not create subscriptions, bundles, coins, gems, battle pass, discount timers, cross-sell carousel, or other purchase products.

### Screen 4 — Ad-free purchase active

- A restrained success confirmation screen/state: **“You’re ad-free”**.
- Confirm: **“Interstitial ads are removed from this account.”**
- Primary button: **Back to Oltivra**.
- Keep it calm and premium; do not use confetti explosions, upgrade tiers, social sharing, forced rating prompt, or new offers.

### Screen 5 — Reconnecting to battle

- Design a small full-screen-safe recovery state layered over a paused/obscured live match, never exposing the answer or future question data.
- Heading: **“Reconnecting to battle…”**
- Supporting text: **“Your match is still being checked.”**
- Show only a calm status indicator and no interactive answer option, score manipulation, retry reward, or player data.
- Do not show a dramatic error, elapsed-time promise, server details, ad, or purchase prompt.

### Screen 6 — Match safely unavailable

- A technical cancellation/recovery outcome for a match that cannot continue safely.
- Heading: **“This battle couldn’t continue”**.
- Supporting text: **“No result was recorded. You can safely return and play again.”**
- Primary action: **Back to Home**.
- Secondary action: **Play again**.
- Do not claim an invented reward, deduct progression, expose backend errors, show an ad, or use blame/shaming language.

### Package guardrails

- An interstitial ad is externally provided. Do not design a custom Oltivra interstitial creative or make a fictional ad page.
- Ads and purchases never interrupt an active question, answer, result visibility, or reconnect recovery. Navigation remains available after the result flow.
- End after these six screens. Do not extend the app with any new feature, screen, package, or design direction.

## END OF STITCH PROMPT
