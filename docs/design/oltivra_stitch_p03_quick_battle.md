# Oltivra — Stitch Package 03: Quick Battle Live Match and Result

## Use

Use only after Package 02 is approved. This package covers the live Quick Battle experience and its direct result state.

---

## START OF STITCH PROMPT

Apply the approved **Oltivra Design Foundation** and preserve every approved component from earlier packages.

Generate **exactly six high-fidelity portrait mobile screens** for **Package 03 — Quick Battle Live Match and Result**. These screens have no bottom navigation. The visual priority is always timer and match status, question, four answers, then score/status.

Use neutral answer cards before resolution. Do not permanently assign red, blue, yellow, and green to answer positions. Use green only for a confirmed correct answer and coral only for a confirmed wrong answer. Keep all reaction controls small and away from questions, answers, timers, and tap targets.

Do not add chat, voice, answer hints, paid aids, revive, currency, leaderboard browsing during play, player profile navigation, bot labels, or an extra match mode.

### Screen 1 — Quick Battle: active question

- Top line: **Quick Battle · Round 3 of 10** with a clear 11-second countdown.
- Show four compact player score chips/avatars. Do not label bots or humans.
- Use one concise factual sample question and exactly four large answer options. Reserve a stable optional media region above the question, but do not use copyrighted celebrity, film, album, or sports imagery.
- A small reaction trigger may sit in a safe top corner. Its opened state is a tiny anchored row of curated reactions such as `GG!`, `🔥`, `👏`, and `😱`; it must not cover content. It is limited to one reaction for the round.
- The active state must feel urgent but not visually chaotic.

### Screen 2 — Quick Battle: local wrong answer locked

- Reuse the same game layout.
- Show one selected answer in coral with a compact **−4** result indicator.
- Other answers are disabled for this player. Keep the countdown active and show that the battle continues.
- Do **not** reveal the correct answer, other players’ selected answers, or the round winner yet.

### Screen 3 — Quick Battle: round closes and answer reveals

- Reuse the same game layout after another participant is first to answer correctly.
- Stop the timer. Show a concise public event such as **“Ava wins +7”**.
- Clearly mark the correct answer green. If this player was wrong, retain their coral selected-answer trace in a subtle way.
- Show a compact updated score row. Do not make this a results screen or add a congratulatory animation.

### Screen 4 — Quick Battle: between-round transition

- A short, calm automatic transition layout, not a new interactive menu.
- Show **“Round 4 of 10 · Medium”**, current ranking/score positions, and a subtle **“Next question incoming”** indication.
- Do not include a Continue button, ads, mission rewards, or player actions.

### Screen 5 — Quick Battle: Sudden Death

- Top status: **Sudden Death** and **“First correct answer wins”**.
- Show only the players tied for first as active; non-tied players are compactly marked **Spectating**.
- Use the same question/answer layout, timer, and controlled reaction placement as Screen 1.
- No normal score calculation, bonus offer, revive, or extra life appears here.

### Screen 6 — Quick Battle: final result

- Clear result hierarchy: player placement, final points, number of question wins, and earned base XP.
- If ranked-eligible, show a small league/weekly progression summary. Do not show raw MMR. The design must also work when no ranked progression is awarded.
- Primary button: **Play Again**.
- Secondary text action: **Home**.
- Include a compact **“Rematch · 00:08”** strip only as a time-limited secondary action for prior human participants; it must not dominate the screen.
- Do not show an interstitial ad, an ad creative, a purchase prompt, or a reward modal in this package. Those are handled later.

### Package guardrails

- The timer, question region, answer region, and player status region remain stable across Screens 1–5.
- Answering must look like a fast, one-handed action with generous touch targets.
- Never reveal future questions or answers, selected answer details of other players, or any bot identity.
- End after these six screens. Do not design Survival, social, profile, settings, or monetisation screens yet.

## END OF STITCH PROMPT
