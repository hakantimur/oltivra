# Oltivra — Stitch Package 04: Survival Live Match and Result

## Use

Use only after Package 03 is approved. This package covers Survival’s distinct elimination and spectator experience.

---

## START OF STITCH PROMPT

Apply the approved **Oltivra Design Foundation** and keep all Quick Battle visual lessons that are shared: stable content regions, large answer targets, no bottom navigation during matches, and controlled reactions.

Generate **exactly six high-fidelity portrait mobile screens** for **Package 04 — Survival Live Match and Result**. Survival must feel tense and premium, never cruel, gambling-like, or dark/cyberpunk.

During an active Survival round, players may see that someone has locked an answer, but must not see correctness, selected answers, correct answer, or elimination until the round resolves. Do not add lives, revive, buyback, paid power-ups, chat, bot labels, or extra player actions.

### Screen 1 — Survival: active question

- Top status: **Survival · Round 5** and a clear 11-second countdown.
- Show **“7 remaining”** prominently but compactly.
- Represent the ten-player roster with small avatar/status marks; do not label bots or humans.
- Show one concise factual question and exactly four large neutral answer choices.
- Keep the question and answers dominant. Use yellow/pink only as restrained Survival energy, not as error/success coding.

### Screen 2 — Survival: answer locked, outcome hidden

- Reuse the Screen 1 layout.
- The player’s selected answer is locked in a neutral selected state; it is not green or coral.
- Show **“Answer locked”** and a calm **“Waiting for the round to resolve…”** state.
- Other roster marks may indicate that another player has locked an answer, but reveal no answer choice or correctness.

### Screen 3 — Survival: round resolution and survived

- The timer is finished and the correct answer is now clearly marked green.
- Show a positive but restrained message: **“You survived”**.
- Update the player roster to show that some players were eliminated only after resolution.
- Include a compact optional all-wrong rescue variation within the layout: **“No one answered correctly. Players who answered are safe. Next round is easier.”** Do not make this a separate screen or invent a penalty.
- The transition to the next round is automatic; no Continue button.

### Screen 4 — Survival: eliminated

- A warm, respectful elimination state: **“You’re out this round.”**
- Show final place so far only if known; do not show shaming language, skull imagery, lives, revive, currency, or ad offer.
- Primary button: **Watch Battle**.
- Secondary text action: **Leave after match**.
- The visual design must make it clear the player cannot answer again.

### Screen 5 — Survival: spectator view

- Top status: **Watching Battle · 3 remaining**.
- Show the current question, timer, compact active roster, and answers as read-only.
- Do not show tappable answer choices or any way to re-enter.
- Permit one small curated reaction trigger per remaining round, placed safely away from the question and answers.
- Include a quiet state label: **“You were eliminated”**.

### Screen 6 — Survival: final result

- Result hierarchy: placement, earned base XP, completed rounds, and a Crown treatment for first place only.
- If ranked-eligible, show a small league/weekly progression summary without raw MMR. The composition must also work when no ranked progression applies.
- Primary button: **Play Again**.
- Secondary text action: **Home**.
- Include a compact 10-second Rematch opportunity only as a secondary strip where applicable.
- Do not show an ad, purchase prompt, reward modal, leaderboard browsing, or a revive option.

### Package guardrails

- Do not reuse Quick Battle’s “first correct answer wins” language in Survival.
- Do not show answer correctness before round resolution.
- Keep all roster/status information legible without pushing the question or answers below the fold.
- End after these six screens. Do not design social, ranking, profile, settings, or monetisation screens yet.

## END OF STITCH PROMPT
